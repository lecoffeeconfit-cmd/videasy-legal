Subscription and billing questions

App Store charges and refunds are handled by Apple. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. For a purchase that does not appear in EditVid, open the Studio Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases.

Visit Apple billing support for help with App Store billing or refund requests.
