1. Who this policy covers

EditVid is a video-editing app operated by Howard Tran. This policy covers information handled through the EditVid app and the online services it uses. It does not cover Apple, third-party apps you choose to share with, or the providers’ own services; those providers have their own policies.

EditVid does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sync your projects to an EditVid cloud account.

2. Your device, videos, and projects

Projects, imported videos and images, captions, transcripts, edits, downloaded sound files, speech models, exports are stored in EditVid’s private app storage on your device. When you select media, EditVid copies it into the app so you can work on it. EditVid does not upload that media, your captions, transcripts, or project files to our servers or to the speech-model provider.

Speech transcription runs on your device after the model is downloaded. You choose when to import media, record with the camera or microphone, save an export to Photos, or share an export with another app. Your chosen destination then handles anything you share under its own policies.

Deleting a project in EditVid removes the project from the app. Uninstalling EditVid may remove app-private project files. Exports saved to Photos, Files, or another service need to be removed there separately.

3. Optional online features

Speech-model downloads

If you choose to download a speech model, the download is served by Hugging Face. The provider receives ordinary request information, such as your IP address and technical details needed to deliver the file. Your video and its audio are not sent for transcription.

Sound search

If you search the optional sound catalog, EditVid sends the words you enter and selected catalog filters to Openverse and Wikimedia Commons to find results. Those services also receive ordinary network request information. If you download a result, the sound file is saved on your device. Each sound remains subject to its creator’s license and attribution requirements.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store payments. EditVid does not receive your full payment-card details. RevenueCat helps retrieve products, validate and restore purchases, and determine whether your Studio Pro access is active. Because EditVid has no login, RevenueCat creates an anonymous app-user identifier when needed. Purchase processing may involve that identifier, product and subscription status, transaction or receipt information, and technical information needed to provide the service.

Your payment and subscription are also subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

5. Crash diagnostics

Release builds send crash and error diagnostics to Sentry when the production diagnostics setting is enabled. These reports can include app and operating-system versions, device context, error messages, stack traces, and technical event identifiers. EditVid configures Sentry without default personal-identifying data, screenshots, view-hierarchy attachments, performance traces, or log events. Query strings are removed from network breadcrumbs.

Sentry is used to understand crashes and improve reliability, not for advertising. See Sentry’s Privacy Policy.

6. Your choices

  • Choose which videos and images to import, and when to use camera, microphone, or Photos features.
  • Skip optional model downloads and sound searches.
  • Delete projects in EditVid and manage exports saved outside the app in their destination.
  • Manage or cancel an App Store subscription through your Apple Account settings. Restore eligible purchases in EditVid.

If you have a privacy question or request, email us. Since EditVid has no account or cloud project library, most project and media controls are available directly on your device. Apple and service providers may retain transaction or diagnostic information under their own policies and legal obligations.

7. Storage, security, and updates

Your project data stays in EditVid’s app storage until you delete it or remove the app. Copies you save or share elsewhere are managed by those destinations. Apple and the providers described in this policy handle their own transaction, network, or diagnostic records under their respective retention and security practices.

We use platform and service-provider safeguards designed to protect information, but no device or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this policy when EditVid or its services change. The date at the top shows when the latest version was published.

8. Questions about privacy?

Howard Tran handles EditVid privacy questions. We’re happy to explain how a feature works or help you find the right device setting.
